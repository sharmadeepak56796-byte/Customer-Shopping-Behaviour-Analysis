# 🛍️ Customer Shopping Behaviour Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer shopping behavior through an interactive Power BI dashboard. The dashboard provides valuable insights into customer demographics, purchasing habits, product performance, subscription trends, and revenue generation.

The objective is to help businesses understand customer preferences and make data-driven decisions that improve customer engagement and increase sales.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

- Analyze customer purchasing patterns.
- Identify top-performing product categories and products.
- Understand customer subscription behavior.
- Evaluate revenue contribution across different customer segments.
- Support business decisions through data visualization and analytics.

---

## 📈 Key Insights
